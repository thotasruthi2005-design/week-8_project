ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Eligibility Criteria

Undergraduate Programs

  • Passed 10+2 or equivalent examination.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks.
  • Required subjects must be studied in qualifying examination.
  • Entrance examination may be required for selected programs.

Postgraduate Programs

  • Recognized bachelor's degree.
  • Minimum qualifying percentage as prescribed by the university.
  • Valid entrance examination score where applicable.
  • Additional requirements may apply to specific programs.
